=====================================================================
                          AFK BOT  v1.9
                  How to install and use it
=====================================================================

---------------------------------------------------------------------
READ THIS FIRST: BAN RISK
---------------------------------------------------------------------
AFK Bot plays Fortnite for you. Epic Games' rules don't allow bots,
macros or AFK farming. Accounts caught using them can be banned, for
a while or for good, and Epic doesn't give back items or V-Bucks.

There's no way to make a bot like this safe. If you can't afford to
lose your account, don't use it. The app asks you to confirm this
every time it starts.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU NEED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Windows 10 or 11
- Fortnite on PC, set to English
- An internet connection (your key is checked online)


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - UNZIP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Right-click AFKBot.zip > Extract All > pick a folder (your Desktop is
fine). Don't run the bot from inside the zip, and keep everything from
the zip together in that folder.

The first time you open AFKBot.exe, Windows may say "Windows protected
your PC". Click "More info" > "Run anyway". Then click Yes when it asks
for admin rights. The bot needs them so its clicks reach Fortnite.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - TESSERACT (THE SCREEN READER)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- If there's a "Tesseract-OCR" folder next to AFKBot.exe, you're done.
- If not, install it from:
      https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki
  Keep the default install folder.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - FORTNITE SETTINGS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Fortnite must be in English. The bot sets the rest itself the first
time it's in the lobby: Window Mode Windowed, your chosen FPS (Match
tab > In-game FPS) and the FPS counter off. It reads each setting
first and only changes what's different. Change your FPS later and it
checks again the next time it's in the lobby.

Optional: tick Match tab > "Also set the other graphics settings" and
it also sets Color Blind Mode Tritanope (strength 5), UI Parallax On,
Dynamic 3D Resolution Off, View Distance Far, Textures Low, Meshes
Low, GPU Crash Debugging Off, NVIDIA Reflex On and Report Performance
Stats Disabled. Settings your PC doesn't have are skipped.

Want to do it by hand instead? Settings > Video: Window Mode Windowed,
Resolution 1280 x 720, Show FPS Off. Then turn off Match tab > "Set
them up in the lobby".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - GET YOUR KEY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open AFKBot.exe, tick the three boxes, click "Accept and continue".
2. Click "Get a key". The key website opens.
3. Pick a route (Lootlabs or Linkvertise) and click "Start
   checkpoint 1". Finish the page, and you come back to the key site
   by yourself. Do any remaining checkpoints the same way.
   Don't rush or skip, or the site makes you redo that checkpoint.
4. Click "Copy key", paste it into the bot, click "Activate".

- The first check can take up to a minute. That's normal, just wait.
- Time left on your key shows bottom-right ("Key: 23h 12m left").
  When it runs out, the bot stops and you get a new key the same way.
- A key works on ONE PC: the first PC that uses it.
- The bot remembers your key, so you don't paste it every time.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - FIRST RUN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Start Fortnite and go to the Battle Royale lobby.
2. In the bot, open the Tools tab and click "Place Fortnite window".
3. Click "Check screen reading". The log at the bottom should say
   "screen = lobby". If it says "unknown", check step 3.
4. Click Start (or press Ctrl+Alt+1).

While it runs, leave Fortnite in front. Don't minimise it or click
into other windows.

Hotkeys (work even while Fortnite is in front):
  Ctrl+Alt+1  start         Ctrl+Alt+P  pause / resume
  Ctrl+Alt+2  stop          Ctrl+Alt+K  close the bot


---------------------------------------------------------------------
BATTLE BUS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
On the bus the bot opens the map (M), finds the bus (the ship icon at
the back of the blue route arrows) and jumps as soon as the bus is
over land. Then it looks straight down and
holds W until it lands ("Dive for" in the Match tab, 30 s by default).
Using a different map key in Fortnite? Change "Map key" in the Match
tab. Turn the whole thing off with "Jump when the bus is over land".

To test it: open the map in Fortnite on the bus, then click
Tools > "Check bus map". The log says whether it found your arrow and
whether it would jump, and debug\bus_map_check.png shows what it saw.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TABS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything saves by itself. The defaults work, so you only need these
if you want to change something.

Match ..... queue automatically, force Zero Build / Squad / Fill,
            Fortnite settings (in-game FPS, hide the FPS counter, the
            other graphics settings),
            human-like movement, sprint (and your sprint key), extra
            jumps, random timings, pickaxe key, camera speed,
            battle bus (jump over land and dive, map key, dive time)
Leaving ... what to do when you're eliminated, leave when few players
            are left (e.g. 20), leave when your whole squad is out
Options ... AFK chat messages, timing, update checks, your key,
            colour theme (presets or your own colours)
Discord ... get updates on your phone (see below)
Tools ..... place the Fortnite window, check screen reading, insert
            another key, open the debug folder, set up Fortnite's
            settings now, Tesseract path


---------------------------------------------------------------------
DISCORD UPDATES (OPTIONAL)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In your own Discord server: Server Settings > Integrations >
   Webhooks > New Webhook > Copy Webhook URL.
2. Paste it into "Webhook link" in the Discord tab, click "Send test".
3. To get pinged: Discord Settings > Advanced > turn on Developer Mode,
   right-click your name > Copy User ID, paste it into "Your Discord
   user ID", click "Test ping".

You get a message when a match ends, when the bot leaves a match, when
something goes wrong, on a timer, "You found <item>, congrats!" when a
reward pops up (with how many of that item you've found), and a session
summary when the bot stops.

Found Pirate Shop Access? You get a big ping with your total hours,
matches and the odds, plus what else you could have done with all that
time. It works for the banner in a match and for the popup in the
lobby (only one ping per find). Turn it off or send a preview with
"Big ping when Pirate Shop Access is found" in the Discord tab.

Screenshots get black bars over player names (yours and your
teammates') so you can share them. Turn it off with "Black out player
names" in the Discord tab.

Want rewards or problems in their own channel? Make a webhook in that
channel and paste it under "Separate channels". Empty = main webhook.
Keep your webhook links private.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The bot checks for a new version when it opens (and every 2 hours
while it's open). If there is one, it asks you: "Update now" downloads
it, restarts the bot and keeps your settings and key. "Later" asks
again next time. While the bot is farming it waits until you stop it.
You can also click "Update to v..." at the top at any time.

Some updates are required. Then the old version can't start any more
and "Update now" is the only way to keep using the bot.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGING YOUR KEY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Tools > "Insert another key" (or click "Key: ..." bottom-right).
Paste the new key and click Activate. The bot keeps running while you
do this and switches over once the new key works. "Back" cancels.

Options > Key > "Log out" forgets your key and stops the bot.
A key stays locked to the first PC that used it, even after logging
out. Changed PCs? Contact support.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEMS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
"That isn't a valid key"
    Copy the whole key again, including "AFK-".
"This key has expired"
    Get a new key with "Get a key".
"This key is already used on another PC"
    Keys work on one PC. Contact support.
"Couldn't reach the key server"
    Check your internet and try again in a minute.
"Tesseract not found"
    Do step 2. If you installed it somewhere else, put the path to
    tesseract.exe in the Tools tab.
The bot clicks but nothing happens in Fortnite
    Close it, right-click AFKBot.exe > Run as administrator.
The screen reads as "unknown" or the bot gets stuck
    Fortnite must be Windowed, 1280 x 720 (or any 16:9 size) and in
    English. Click "Place Fortnite window" again. Still stuck? Turn on
    Options > "Save debug screenshots", let it get stuck once, then
    send the pictures from Tools > "Open debug folder" to support.
Players left shows "-"
    Turn off the FPS / network overlay in Fortnite (the bot turns
    the FPS counter off itself unless you untick that option).
The bot couldn't set Fortnite's settings
    Go to the lobby and click Tools > "Fortnite settings". If it
    still fails, send debug\settings_not_found.png or
    settings_unset.png to support.
The bot doesn't jump off the bus
    Open the map on the bus and click Tools > "Check bus map", then
    send debug\bus_map_check.png to support.
The camera spins too much or too little
    Tools > tick "Show advanced tools" > "Test camera" should turn
    about a quarter turn. Change Options > Camera speed until it does.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIVACY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Key checks send the key server your key and a scrambled PC ID (it
  can't be turned back into your name or files). The server also sees
  your IP address, like any website.
- Discord messages only go to the webhook YOU set up.
- Your settings (config.json), stats (stats.json) and debug pictures
  stay in the bot's folder on your PC.
